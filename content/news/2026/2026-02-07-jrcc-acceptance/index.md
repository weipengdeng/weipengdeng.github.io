---
title: "Paper on Ghost Kitchens accepted by Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services"
date: 2026-02-07
---

I am pleased to share that our paper, **"Digital visibility, physical obscurity: Uncovering the location strategies of ghost kitchens in platform urbanism"**, has been accepted for publication in the **Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services**.

In this study, we analyze the spatial patterns and location logics of ghost kitchens in Shenzhen by integrating multi-source platform data and urban metrics. The findings show how platform-mediated food services can trade storefront visibility for delivery efficiency, and offer planning and governance implications for consumer protection, food safety, and public health.

This work is a collaborative effort with **Yihong Tang**, **Yaxin Liu**, and **Prof. Tianren Yang**. I sincerely thank my co-authors, the editor [Prof. Soora Rasouli](https://www.tue.nl/en/research/researchers/soora-rasouli), and the anonymous reviewers for their constructive comments and support.
